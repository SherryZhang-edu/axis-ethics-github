THE CASE FILE

Two weeks before launch.

Safety × evidence × responsibility

You are Lena, the perception lead at Momentum Dynamics. Your team is preparing AXIS, a Level 4 autonomous driving system, for release in specific, pre-mapped urban environments.

Failure conditionsHeavy rain + low light

Pedestrians wearing dark clothing are more likely to be misclassified.

Reported test result≈ 1 in 1,000

Failure-to-brake events in the described simulations. The denominator needs scrutiny.

Commercial pressureLaunch now. Fix later?

The manager proposes an update one or two months after release.

Read the full case

The dilemma

Lena must decide how to respond to a known failure mode while facing pressure to launch. The manager claims that AXIS is safer than human drivers overall. The case does not provide the comparative data needed to evaluate that claim independently. Consider what this claim does—and does not—establish about the specific failure conditions.

02
IDENTIFY THE STAKEHOLDERS

Who carries the consequences?

To do

Identify at least three stakeholders. Explain what each stands to gain or lose; be specific about safety, access, employment, money or trust.

Consider whose voice is missing

Think about pedestrians, passengers, people exposed to the failure conditions, the engineering team, managers, the company and public oversight bodies.

03
APPLY THE IEEE CODE

Connect principles to facts.

To do

Choose at least two relevant clauses; three is a useful target. For each, explain the evidence, the ethical connection and the action it supports. Do not assume every clause applies.
